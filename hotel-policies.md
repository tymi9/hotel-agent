# Hotel Estrella, Barcelona: guest policies

Invented for the course. Each section is complete on its own.

## Cancellation

Flexible rate: free cancellation until 18:00 local time on the day before arrival. After that, the first night is charged.

Non-refundable rate: no refund on cancellation or no-show. The stay may be moved once to new dates within 6 months, subject to availability, for a €25 change fee.

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 15:00. Check-out is by 11:00.

Late check-out until 14:00 costs €20 and depends on availability; the guest asks reception on the day.

Early check-in cannot be guaranteed. Luggage can be stored free of charge from 09:00.

## Pets

Dogs up to 15 kg are welcome in Double and Family rooms for €15 per night. No pets in Suites. Guide dogs are always welcome, free of charge, in all rooms.

## Breakfast

Buffet breakfast is served from 07:00 to 10:30. It costs €14 per person per day when added at check-in, or €12 when added to the booking in advance. Children under 6 eat free.

## Parking

On-site parking costs €18 per night. No reservation is possible. Electric-vehicle charging: two chargers, first come first served, €0.40 per kWh.

## Payment

Flexible rates are charged at check-out. Non-refundable rates are charged at booking. Invoices for companies can be issued at check-out if the company details are given before departure.

## Escalation

Escalate to a member of staff (do not answer yourself) when:
- the guest asks for a refund or compensation above €300 that the policies above would allow or do not clearly settle;
- the guest mentions a safety, health or legal issue;
- the guest complains about a member of staff;
- the guest asks for anything the policies above do not cover.

When a policy clearly answers the question (for example, a non-refundable rate cannot be refunded), explain the policy. That is not an escalation.
